# PROJECT_CONTEXT.md

## Project Name

yehiakamal.com

## Website Owner

Yehia Kamal El-Din

## Professional Role

Chief Accountant

## Location

Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

## Website Purpose

This website is a personal executive portfolio and online resume.

Its purpose is to present Yehia Kamal El-Din as a credible, experienced, and professional finance/accounting leader.

The website should help with:
- Job opportunities
- HR screening
- Professional branding
- Sharing CV and cover letter
- Displaying certificates and experience
- Building trust before interviews

## Target Audience

The main audience includes:
- HR managers
- Recruiters
- Finance managers
- CFOs
- Business owners
- Accounting and consulting firms

## Brand Positioning

The website should position Yehia as:

A senior accounting professional with 12+ years of experience across Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, and Egypt, with strong expertise in financial reporting, payroll, budgeting, government platforms, and accounting team leadership.

## Current Website Structure

The current website is a one-page static portfolio with these sections:

- Hero
- About
- Expertise
- Experience
- Certificates
- Education
- Documents
- Contact

## Current Strengths

- Premium executive visual identity
- Dark and gold color palette
- Clear timeline experience
- Certificate gallery
- CV and cover letter downloads
- Simple static structure
- Easy to host and maintain

## Main Improvement Goals

Future improvements should focus on:

1. SEO
2. Accessibility
3. Mobile navigation
4. Performance
5. Stronger professional branding
6. Better certificate naming
7. Adding achievements
8. Adding selected projects
9. Improving HR conversion
10. Keeping the project simple

## Strategic Direction

This website should not become a complex web application.

It should become a strong, elegant, fast, and professional personal brand website for a finance executive.

## Development Philosophy

Document before changing.

Improve gradually.

One sprint at a time.

No random redesigns.

No unnecessary frameworks.

No complexity unless it clearly improves the website.
